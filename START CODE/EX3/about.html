Meet Our Club President

We are delighted to welcome you to CC, Catminton Club!

CC aims to be the premier destination for badminton enthusiasts in the area.

CC has 3 missions to accomplish:

  • Be the #1 club with the most fearsome badminton players in the cat universe
  • Conquer every opposing teams/clubs that go against us on the court
  • Become a fun and welcoming community for all players
